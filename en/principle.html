Principles

Not just a slogan, but a training path built around long-term retention

This page focuses on four ideas: when the brain is more ready to encode information, how information is packaged into memory, why acoustic stimulation may help guide the learning state, and why lasting retention depends on later consolidation and retraining.

Core idea

Theta rhythm at 4–8 Hz is treated as an important window for memory encoding

The uploaded principle document organizes the logic into four connected lines: theta rhythm and memory encoding, theta-gamma coupling, external acoustic guidance, and memory consolidation during sleep.

Training flow

Connect state, encoding, consolidation, and retraining into one system

AI Brainwave English works best when it is understood as a system, not as a single device.

01

Enter a state that supports sustained input

The learning cabin, headset, content pacing, and training environment are meant to reduce distraction and improve continuity.

02

Move new words from exposure toward encoding

The theta and theta-gamma logic helps explain why memorization is not only about repetition, but also about timing and organization.

03

Use staged review to reinforce learning

The system tracks learning behavior, mastery, and review progress so that follow-up checks are more data-based.

04

Support retention with consolidation and retraining

This is why later review and free retraining matter. Long-term retention is a process, not a single moment.

A principles page should explain, not just promote

The purpose is to help families and schools understand what the system is actually doing and why the training logic is organized this way.

What matters most

It is not magic, and it is not just a headset

The real logic is the system: device, environment, content, pacing, review, testing, consolidation, and retraining working together as one closed loop.

Not a one-time triggerIt aims at a longer retention path, not instant memorization.
Not separate from courseworkIt needs to work with phonics, vocabulary goals, and coaching.
Not only about hardwareThe method and the retention cycle are what make the system coherent.
