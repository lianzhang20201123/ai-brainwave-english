Service period & rights

One payment, with a three-year retraining window

The standard service period is six months, including access to the training system, learning hardware, coaching support and the private study group. Retraining can be booked within three years if consolidation is still needed.

Best taken after a free assessment

Families can start with phonics and vocabulary testing, then choose the right entry point and training rhythm more efficiently.
