Who Is AI Brainwave English Best For?

Families often ask whether the system is only for junior-high students. In reality, the program structure already covers multiple stages, from upper primary school to adult test preparation.

What this article helps parents understand

AI Brainwave English supports students and adults from upper primary school to IELTS and TOEFL preparation.

Which age groups fit best?

  • Upper primary school for pre-junior-high vocabulary preparation.
  • Junior high for Zhongkao-oriented vocabulary reinforcement and reading support.
  • High school for core Gaokao vocabulary growth.
  • University students and adults for CET-4, CET-6, postgraduate, TOEFL and IELTS needs.

Who should consider a trial first?

  • Learners who keep forgetting words after memorization.
  • Learners who get stuck on vocabulary while reading.
  • Learners who work hard but still improve too slowly.
  • Families who want a calmer, more structured vocabulary solution.

Why start with a free assessment?

  • It shows the learner’s phonics and vocabulary baseline.
  • It helps choose the right entry point and program level.
  • It reduces blind enrollment and improves fit.

Yes. The programs also cover CET-4, CET-6, postgraduate preparation, TOEFL and IELTS.
