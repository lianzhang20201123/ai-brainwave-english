What Is the Most Effective Way to Build Gaokao Vocabulary?

Buying a thicker word book is rarely the solution. A better route is to stabilize high-frequency core words and improve how well they stay over time.

What this article helps parents understand

Building Gaokao vocabulary works best when students focus on core words, review rhythm and long-term retention.

What weakens Gaokao vocabulary work?

  • Trying to cover too much too quickly.
  • Using mechanical copying without enough retrieval and review.
  • Studying new words constantly while old ones disappear.

A more effective method

  • Rank vocabulary by exam relevance and frequency.
  • Combine reading and vocabulary instead of separating them completely.
  • Track retention after several days and weeks, not just same-day performance.

What parents should monitor

  • Is reading becoming smoother?
  • Is writing using stronger and more accurate words?
  • Is the learner retaining more after one or two weeks?

Frequently asked questions

Before Gaokao, is more vocabulary always better?

No. Priority and retention rate matter more than raw volume.

Why is vocabulary always tied to Gaokao English improvement?

Because reading, cloze, writing and listening all depend on vocabulary.
