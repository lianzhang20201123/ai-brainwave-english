Pilot model

Partners need an executable operating package, not just a concept

The partnership conversation should focus on a small pilot: local leads, parent communication, coach training, cabin scheduling, exit testing and review records.

Space and equipment

Learning cabins, headsets, testing area and parent-consultation flow.

Training and SOP

Coach, consultant and operations scripts for a repeatable service process.

Lead materials

Assessment entry, short-video scripts, parent Q&A and trial-campaign materials.

Data review

Lead source, appointment rate, trial completion, exit test, conversion and referral tracking.

Who fits

Centers with local parent access and a delivery team should test first

This model may fit tutoring centers, ESL centers, after-school programs, test-prep organizations and city-level channel partners that can run assessment, trial and parent communication.
