FAQ

The questions franchise prospects ask most

Can I join without education-industry experience?

Yes. The uploaded franchise document explicitly positions this as a fully managed model with headquarters assigning the store manager, sales and coaches.

Is the hardware safe?

The material describes the technology as non-invasive acoustic stimulation and points visitors to safety testing information.

What if the location loses money?

The document highlights a risk-sharing mechanism in which losses are not allocated in the same way before payback is reached.

How long is the contract and when is the deposit returned?

The uploaded material describes a five-year contract and a full deposit return at the end of the term if there is no breach.
